## Management intent and evidence limits

The manager approved the framework unchanged, recognizing greater real-world value in a value-maximizing model while accepting lower implementation-cost concessions for this experiment. A deferred high-value initiative can remain attractive for a real operating company. The objective here is a credible, manageable experiment, not a claim that inexpensive reporting universally creates the greatest business value.

The original portfolio supplies gross addressable benefit hypotheses, not demonstrated incremental benefits. Its dollar bands alone do not satisfy the approved value floor: native reports, workbook/process changes, inability to act, or partial realization could erase the opportunity. A fictional company cannot provide real interviews or operating evidence. Public product documentation would not establish what an unspecified Harborline ERP already does.

To make Gate 2 decision-ready, the next section proposes three explicit scenario baselines for manager adoption. **Advance is recommended only if the manager accepts the corresponding scenario premise at Gate 2.** This does not report an existing unknown as verified or change the framework's value floor. If a premise is declined or remains unspecified, that candidate is Needs Discovery and is excluded from scoring until a subsequent Gate 2 decision. All three can be rejected without breaking the experiment.

## Judgment calls for Gate 2

- **Adopt fictional premises versus request more discovery:** the three proposed survivors are defensible only as explicitly accepted experiment scenarios. A stricter evidence requirement leaves OPP-01/02/04/05/06/07 in Needs Discovery and yields no survivors today. That is a legitimate alternative; neither route produces empirical business validation.
- **Small source footprint versus clearer cross-system value:** OPP-01/02 may be easier to build, but native alternatives could eliminate their purpose. OPP-04 has a clearer integration question but additional delivery risk. This is a comparison to evaluate after Gate 2, not a ranking now.
- **Reject OPP-03 versus redefine it:** reject the current convenience-oriented framing. A concrete unserved sales action can justify a revised candidate; inventing that action merely to retain a cheap project would defeat the value floor.
- **Defer OPP-08 versus investigate for value:** the manager's recognition of the value-maximizing model matters particularly here. Its broader real-world upside remains in the portfolio; the deferral reflects this experiment's accepted delivery/ownership concession.
- **Freight/returns/inventory versus the proposed survivor set:** these may be stronger real-world opportunities. Current semantic/ownership gaps warrant explicit discovery rather than attaching a high benefit number to a poorly bounded MVP.

Recommendation: approve OPP-01, OPP-02 and OPP-04 for detailed prioritization **with SCN-01, SCN-02 and SCN-04 explicitly adopted as fictional comparison premises**, retain the other dispositions, and keep all readiness/value limitations. The final choice remains Gate 3; no project is recommended for implementation here. See the [Gate 2 decision package](../gates/gate-2-triage-review.md).
