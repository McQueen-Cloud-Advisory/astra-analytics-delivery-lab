The learning comparison is deliberately unweighted: all three can exercise contracts, synthesis, independent expected results, pipelines, exception reporting and recovery. **No evidence establishes that 02 teaches more than 01 or produces a better agent-capability experiment.** The extra $7,200 of fictional annual opportunity is a company-context tie-break, not actual lab value. The human-time ranges overlap; a preference for the lowest forecast burden favors 01, while either domain is defensible.

Percentages divide the rounded component endpoints by $30 and round to whole percentages. **They compare against the whole allowance, not verified remaining headroom.** Existing GCP consumption, the exact allowance/credit terms, billing and attributable charges remain unverified. The nominal remaining amount is approximately `$30 − existing monthly consumption`; it may be much less than $30. GCP project existence is manager-reported, not evidence of a tested authentication, permission or billing path. The allowance is not a proven hard cap or authority to provision resources.

The clarification removes hosted Power BI publishing from this endpoint and the standing Pro subscription from incremental project costing. Local report connection, rendering and refresh still require verification. Separately billed optional services, hardware or access remediation are not assumed necessary or free. Total actual project charges remain unmeasured. 02's continuing lab ownership forecast is **0.5–1 human hour/month plus incident/change effort**; no equivalent measured ongoing burden exists for any candidate.

Because 01 and 02 share the B1 estimate, the current evidence does **not** support choosing between them to save GCP money. 04's upper component estimate exceeds the entire nominal allowance before existing usage, making its cloud fit less comfortable. These are comparison workloads, not minimum costs: a smaller fixture, lower refresh frequency or shorter retention could reduce any candidate's usage if later approved and verified. No architecture or optimization result is claimed here.
